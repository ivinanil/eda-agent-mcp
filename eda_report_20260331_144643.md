### Key Observations:
1. **The `arrival_year` × `arrival_month` correlation (–0.34) is a data artefact**, not a real seasonal effect. It results from incomplete year coverage — 2017 entries begin partway through the year. This must be accounted for in any time-series split or seasonal feature engineering.

2. **The three booking history variables** (`repeated_guest`, `no_of_previous_cancellations`, `no_of_previous_bookings_not_canceled`) form a loosely correlated cluster. While no single pair exceeds 0.7, using all three together in a linear model risks soft multicollinearity. Consider creating a single **"guest loyalty score"** composite feature.

3. **No strong direct correlation** exists between `lead_time` and other numeric features, which is surprising given its expected relationship with cancellations. This suggests the lead time–cancellation relationship is likely **non-linear** and better captured by tree-based models.
